# IOI GHG Tracker (v11) — see DEPLOY.md to go live

A working test build of the GHG data-entry and calculation app. Replaces the manual monthly
re-tallying in "Calculadora GEI para IOI 2025" with structured entry and automatic calculation,
organized around how IOI actually tracks people: Faculty & Leaders, Groups, Individuals, and
IOI Staff.

## What's in this version

- **Participants tab** — Faculty & Leaders, Groups, and Individuals (sample data standing in
  for live Embark records), with search/filter and collapsed-by-default cards so the list stays
  usable at 100+ participants. Every participant automatically gets: a mainland↔Baltra flight
  (fixed at medium tier), the full local-transport chain (bus/barge/bus/ferry/taxi, round trip),
  and nights calculated from arrival/departure dates. Faculty & Leaders and Groups get 1
  mainland-hotel night + the rest at the Galápagos destination (hotel, by default). Individuals
  get NO mainland night — 100% of their nights are in Galápagos, using whichever accommodation
  type is on their record (homestay, volunteer house, or hotel — all three are valid options).
  Every leg and every night entry is individually editable or removable for exceptions, and you
  can add a flight manually per person instead of relying on Embark sync. If a participant's
  Embark dates change after their auto-records were generated, their card shows a "needs
  review" flag rather than silently recalculating over any manual edits.
- **IOI Staff tab** — entered manually (staff aren't in Embark): flights with a "reason for
  travel" field, hotel nights, and local transport in Galápagos.
- **Emissions & Mitigation Monthly tab** — utilities, waste, and mitigation (crop harvests,
  regenerative agriculture hectares, beach clean-up), with a checklist at the top showing which
  categories already have an entry for the selected month (✅/❌) so staff can see at a glance
  what's still missing. Entries can be corrected (amount) or deleted, not just added.
- **Report tab** — gross/controllable/net emissions, participant-weeks, by-category breakdown,
  and two charts (emissions by source, gross vs. controllable tons/week by category).

Every emission factor lives in `data/factors.js`, each commented with the spreadsheet row it
came from.

## How data flows — and where you edit it

**This app is meant to be the place you edit and correct data — not the spreadsheet.** The
planned Google Sheets output (still to be built) is a generated snapshot for
sharing/backup, not something you'd hand-edit; changes made there wouldn't feed back into the
app. Everything editable lives here: flights, nights, local transport, and monthly entries all
have Remove/Save controls now.

**Embark ↔ app sync is one-way (Embark → app).** Embark stays the system of record for
participant identity, dates, and accommodation type. GHG-specific detail — edited/removed
auto-legs, "this group always uses homestay" exceptions, manually-added flights — lives only in
this app as an overlay on top of the Embark record, and is *not* pushed back to Embark. Trying
to keep two systems in sync bidirectionally is where data quality problems usually creep in;
one-way sync with local overrides is simpler and safer. When you send the list of
always-homestay groups, that becomes a rule applied in this app (e.g. a flag on the group
record), not a change requested in Embark.

**What happens when Embark data changes after the fact (e.g. someone edits a participant's
dates)?** Two different things happen depending on what changed:

- Core fields (name, dates, accommodation type, category) refresh from Embark every time the
  app loads participant data — not a one-time import. So the app always reflects Embark's
  latest values for those fields.
- The *derived* records (auto-generated flights, nights, local transport) are NOT silently
  regenerated when dates change, because staff may have already corrected them manually — a
  silent overwrite would destroy that work. Instead, the app stamps the dates each participant's
  auto-records were calculated from, and flags a mismatch as "needs review" right on their card,
  with a one-click "Regenerate from new dates" button that recalculates fresh (and clearly warns
  it discards manual edits first). Nothing recalculates behind your back — you decide when.

This is implemented and testable now even without live Embark: `_autoGeneratedFor` on each
participant record in `data/store.json` is the stamped snapshot; `needsReview` is computed by
comparing it to the participant's current dates every time `/api/participants` is called.

## Two things confirmed from your last message

- **Local transport already includes the return trip.** Each of the 5 legs in the chain
  (`data/factors.js` → `LOCAL_TRANSPORT_LEGS`) is computed as one-way-kg × 2 — see
  `calcLocalTransportLeg` in `lib/calc.js`.
- **Mainland and Galápagos hotel nights use the identical hotel factor** — they're just tracked
  as separate records (`location: "mainland"` vs `"galapagos"`) so you can see/adjust them
  independently (e.g. a group that stays in a mainland hotel twice).

## Running it

```bash
cd ioi-ghg-app
npm install
npm start
```

Then open **http://localhost:3000** in a browser.

Data is stored in `data/store.json` (a plain JSON file) — fine for testing, but the first
thing to swap out for a real deployment is a proper database, so multiple people can use it
concurrently without file-write conflicts.

## Sanity-checking the math

`data/factors.js` and `lib/calc.js` mirror the spreadsheet's own formulas — flight tiers,
local-transport legs (rows 44-49/51-56), and mitigation crop factors (rows 70-81) are all
computed from the same per-unit constants read directly out of the original workbook's cell
formulas, not retyped by hand.
