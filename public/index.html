IOI GHG Tracker

Welcome to the IOI GHG Tracker

This tool replaces our manual emissions spreadsheet. You enter what happened; it does the calculations and feeds the annual GHG report. This page tells you what to do and when.

Where to start

Participants

Faculty & Leaders, Groups and Individuals. Flights, local transport and nights are generated automatically from their dates. You only check them and fix exceptions.

Monthly routine (ground staff)

  1. Open Emissions & Mitigation Monthly.
  2. Select the month and year in the Monthly checklist. ✅ means already entered, ❌ means still missing.
  3. Enter each missing item using the form below the checklist (use the units shown beside each field).
  4. Check the checklist again: everything should be green before you finish.
  5. Made a mistake? Edit the amount in the entries table and click Save, or click Remove.

Checking participants

  1. Open Participants; use the search box or category filter to find someone, then click their name to expand.
  2. Review flights, local transport and nights. Anything automatic is labelled “(auto)”.
  3. For exceptions (a group with different logistics, someone who skipped a leg), click Remove on that line or add what is missing: Add flight manually or Add nights.
  4. To change a number of nights, click Edit, change the figure, then click Save. If you do not click Save, nothing changes.
  5. If you see an amber needs review tag, the person's dates changed in Embark after the figures were calculated. Check them, or click Regenerate from new dates (this discards manual edits for that person).

Good to know

  • Always edit here, not in a spreadsheet. Any spreadsheet export is a snapshot only.
  • Mainland ↔ Baltra flights are assumed for every participant and are always counted at the medium emissions tier.
  • Local transport is already a round trip. Do not enter the return separately.
  • Nights come from arrival/departure dates. Mainland and Galápagos nights use the same hotel factor but are kept as separate records.
  • Controllable emissions = gross emissions minus participant flights. Staff and international flights count as controllable.
  • Participants come from Embark (imported by the admin); if someone is missing, use “Add a participant manually”.
  • Unsure about something? Ask Daniel (daniel@ioi.ngo) before changing it.

Embark connection

Import now: in Embark, export your participants report as CSV and choose the file here. You get a preview first; nothing changes until you click Apply. Re-importing is safe: people are matched, updated, and never duplicated. Columns are recognised by name (name, category, institution/group, arrival/start date, departure/end date, housing, agency/booking source, flights as e.g. MAD>UIO; UIO>MAD).

Automatic sync: once Embark gives us API access, it is switched on in Render with the settings listed in DEPLOY.md, and the app then refreshes itself every hour.
